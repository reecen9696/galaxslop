Load your game files

This page runs Super Mario Galaxy from your own disc. Nothing is uploaded: the files are copied into this browser's private storage once, then the game starts from there.

  1. Dump your disc and extract it in Dolphin (right-click the game, Properties, Filesystem, Extract Entire Disc).
  2. Convert it with tools/cook/cook.py (see docs/GETTING_YOUR_DISC.md).
  3. Pick the converted folder (the one with manifest.json in it).
